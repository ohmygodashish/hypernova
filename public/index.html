This browser can't reach the mouse

The page talks to the mouse through WebHID, which only Chromium-based desktop browsers support. Open this page in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave or Arc on a computer.

Connect your mouse

Plug in the mouse with its cable, or plug in its 4K dongle. Then click Connect and choose the mouse in the list your browser shows.

  • Close the Cosmic Byte app first. It can overwrite changes you make here.
  • After you unplug the mouse or restart the browser, click Connect again. The browser can't remember this mouse.
  • On Linux, install the udev rule first. Linux setup
